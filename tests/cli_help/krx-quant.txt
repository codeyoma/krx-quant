                        refuses and skips none

                        days and store the promotion list

                        each algorithm that waits for it (#298), and judge it
                        at once (safe beside the engine)
    rollback            put an algorithm back one step, on the model it ran
                        before (only that one is kept, #361)
    retire              retire an algorithm: it is no longer run; its records
                        stay
    unretire            put a retired algorithm back on probation
    trade               trade 005930 on the cells the cost map passes, in

                        (KRX_TRADER_MODE=mock, #405) or on the live account

                        (KRX_TRADER_MODE=live, #408), until interrupted;
                        resume: lift the screen's pause; rearm: re-arm the
                        cumulative loss limit (#396)
    refit-policy        fit the trader's policy again (#399): tonight's cost

                        champion

                        price
