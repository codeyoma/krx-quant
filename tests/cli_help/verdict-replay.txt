usage: krx-quant verdict-replay [-h] [--day DAY] [--days DAYS]
                                [--version {1,2}]

options:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
  --day DAY        the last day replayed, YYYY-MM-DD (default: the latest
                   trading day whose session is over)
  --days DAYS      the recorded trading days replayed (default 20)
  --version {1,2}  the verdict's version (#697): 2 pools each day's ranking
                   members (default), 1 every forecast
